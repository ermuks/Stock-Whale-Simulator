# Sticky note handwriting sources

Downloaded on 2026-11-15 from the official Google Fonts repository.

- Nanum Pen Script Regular — Sandoll / NHN. Korean and Latin.
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nanumpenscript
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/nanumpenscript/NanumPenScript-Regular.ttf
  SHA-256: 6f0d1ab29c7894010dc88831fb7a0a51edb79136e450344183de5b1a8b52bd43
- Yomogi Regular — The Yomogi Project Authors / Satsuyako. Japanese handwriting fallback.
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/yomogi
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/yomogi/Yomogi-Regular.ttf
  SHA-256: ec280f473a03187292905618f24fdb08a6dcbf8620b52637e3b589fe745259b9

Both unmodified font files are under SIL Open Font License 1.1. The original license and copyright files are included beside the TTFs and in Assets/StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyLicenses for player distribution.
Generated TMP assets are named Paper Handwriting KR Latin and Paper Handwriting JP.
The TTFs are not merged, subsetted, or renamed internally.

Validation/StickyNotes/font-coverage.json records actual Unicode cmap coverage.
Nanum contains all 11,172 modern Hangul syllables and 95 printable ASCII characters. Yomogi supplies the Japanese glyphs, including the kana and kanji used by the Japanese note copy.
This does not claim coverage of every rare historical kanji.
